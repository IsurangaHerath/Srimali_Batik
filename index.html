About Srimali Batik

Preserving the art of traditional Sri Lankan batik for generations

Welcome to Srimali Batik, where tradition meets elegance. Our journey began with a passion for preserving the ancient art of batik making, a craft that has been an integral part of Sri Lankan culture for centuries.

Each piece in our collection is meticulously handcrafted by skilled artisans, using time-honored techniques passed down through generations.
